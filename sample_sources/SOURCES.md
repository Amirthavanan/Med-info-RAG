# Authoritative drug-label sources

Use current official labeling when testing this application.

## DailyMed — U.S. National Library of Medicine

DailyMed provides current labeling submitted to FDA by companies, including prescription and OTC drug labels, patient labeling, and prescribing information.

https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/

## FDA Online Label Repository

https://labels.fda.gov/

## FDA drug-labeling resources

https://www.fda.gov/drugs/development-resources/labeling-information-drug-products

### Suggested test drugs

Search DailyMed for:
- Ibuprofen

Download a current PDF label from the individual DailyMed result and upload it into MedInfo RAG.

IMPORTANT:
DailyMed states that its "in use" labeling is the most recent labeling submitted to FDA by companies and may not be identical to the latest FDA-approved labeling. For the most current approved U.S. labeling, cross-check Drugs@FDA/FDA resources.
